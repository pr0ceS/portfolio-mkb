## The problem

Shopify is quick to start with, but you pay for it every month, and again for each app. You also cannot change everything. The checkout in particular is hard to adjust.

The goal here was a webshop on its own stack: faster, cheaper and fully adjustable, including the checkout.

## The approach

The shop is headless. That means the part the customer sees and the part that handles products and orders are separate systems that talk through an API. Each part can be changed or replaced without touching the other.

### Backend

The backend is Medusa v2, an open source e-commerce engine. It keeps the products, prices, carts and orders, with PostgreSQL as the database and Redis next to it. It runs on a Hetzner server that costs about $7 per month.

### Storefront

The storefront is built with Astro 5 and rendered on the server. It runs on Cloudflare Workers, so pages are served from a location close to the visitor. Content is managed in Sanity, a CMS, so texts and images can change without a developer. Search runs on Orama.

### Payments

Payments go through Mollie, with Apple Pay and iDEAL among the methods. No Mollie integration for Medusa was ready for production, so the shop has its own.

## The result

The webshop is live at freddys-store.com and loads very fast. There is full control over the checkout and the tracking. The hosting costs are a fraction of what Shopify plus apps would cost.
