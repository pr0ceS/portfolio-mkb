## The approach

- A room status automation failed without any error. It called a US endpoint instead of the EU one, and it wrote to a link table instead of the room itself.
- A one-off backfill function, with a dry-run mode, that fills in missing financial contacts on customer records.
- A “ticket open for two weeks” rule that fired after a little over three days.
- In Google Ads and Tag Manager: tags that fired twice, a container that also ran inside a form iframe and counted page views up to four times, and dead Universal Analytics tags. I cleaned up the conversion goals as well.
