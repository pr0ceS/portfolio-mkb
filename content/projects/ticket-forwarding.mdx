## The problem

IT tickets sent to the external IT partner arrived in their system (Autotask) as “Company Unknown”.

## The approach

I analysed the timeline of the automation chain step by step. It worked for an administrator and failed for regular staff: field permissions emptied the field in their emails. The same cause was behind a second bug, where tickets were assigned to the wrong team. I rewrote those functions.

## The result

Both bugs are solved for good. And a fixed debugging rule: when it works for me and not for others, check permissions first.
