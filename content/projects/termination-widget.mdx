## The problem

For a termination, staff had to work out by hand when a tenant was allowed to leave, with notice periods, renewal periods and running renewals. That often went wrong.

## The approach

Starting a termination became a step in the CRM process, with my own widget hosted on Cloudflare. The rules are in the widget: only per the first of the month, the notice period must be respected, and a renewal that has not started yet may be interrupted. Exceptions need a mandatory reason. I tested it with a wide set of scenarios, including extreme contracts.

## The result

Wrong termination dates can no longer be entered, and every exception is accounted for.
