## The problem

The rental process runs from lead to viewing, proposal, agreement and invoicing, in a CRM workflow without AI. The question was where the waiting time is and which steps AI could speed up.

## The approach

I rebuilt the workflow one to one as a state machine in React and TypeScript, including required fields and roles. Each phase got an AI assist layer. The rule: the AI proposes, the state machine decides. The AI fills in fields or drafts an email, a person approves.

## The result

An interactive prototype to try AI features safely next to the live CRM.
