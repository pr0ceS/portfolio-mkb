## What it is

A group of Shopify webshops, built and scaled, that are currently active in 12 countries.

## The work

Each shop has a custom theme, built in Liquid, the template language of Shopify. A shop in another country is more than a translation: the texts, the payment methods and the delivery promises have to fit that market.

Recurring work around orders and products is automated, so a shop can grow without the daily work growing along with it.

Customers come in through advertising on Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest.

## What came out of it

Most of the software on this site started as a problem in these shops. The flood of customer service emails led to Aiders. Launching products and managing advertising led to the automation tools described in the other e-commerce cases.
